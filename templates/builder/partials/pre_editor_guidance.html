Before you edit

Three quick wins in the editor

When your site opens, start with these three changes. They make the draft feel like yours right away.

  1. 1
    Change the main headline

    Click the hero headline and rewrite it in your own words.

  2. 2
    Replace one image

    Swap a placeholder photo for something that matches your business.

  3. 3
    Preview mobile

    Use the mobile device view so the layout looks right on a phone.

We do not mark these tasks complete from outside the editor. Open your site when you are ready.
